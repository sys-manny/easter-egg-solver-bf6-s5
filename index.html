    Field instructions

    1. Set every tower to green

    Observe all eight colors first. A press cycles its own tower and both numbered neighbors, wrapping between 8 and 1. Solve, then follow the suggested presses in game. Mark each press here only after doing it in game. Recheck all eight lights before entering the bunker.

    2. Open the bunker and read monitors
    1. Enter the large hole between E and F. Follow the tunnel toward F.
    2. Behind the cylindrical device, use the green door panel.
    3. Go down the stairs, turn left, follow the corridor right, and open the control-room door.
    4. Activate the green-lit radio to turn on the monitors. The “intruders underground” Morse message needs no decoding.
    5. Record the lit lights above all eight shape monitors. Count lit lights, not their positions.
    3. Enter the final sequence

    The large displays give the shape order: open circle, triangle, square, diamond; then filled circle, triangle, square, diamond. Return outside and press those tower numbers in that exact order, including repeats. The last correct press triggers the UFO event and Base Invaders charm.

    Do not run the color solver between these final presses.

    Solo setup and troubleshooting

    The video uses Portal experience 1X4AP, password protected, and skips Vegas Strip to Area 51 through admin controls. A helicopter helps reach tower 6. Other players can change tower colors; record a fresh set if your observations no longer match. If no solution exists, recheck your actual colors instead of inventing new observations.
